The journey

My Story

My first memory of being interested in electronics in particular is taking the back panel off of my guitar amplifier and seeing all of the vacuum tubes, transformers, and resistor/capacitor networks, which then inspired me to learn how it works under the hood. At the same time, I was attending Upper Saint Clair High School in Pittsburgh, PA, where I was taking technical classes that reinforced my love for engineering.

I started my engineering journey at Purdue as a First Year Engineering student considering Nuclear, Computer, and Electrical Engineering. When it was time to decide what specific discipline I wanted to pursue, I chose Computer Engineering because I liked the idea of having a mix of the skillsets possessed by both electrical engineers and computer scientists. During the Spring 2026 semester, I signed up for Digital Signal Processing (ECE 43800) by accident, confusing it with Computer Design and Prototyping (ECE 43700). Instead of dropping DSP, I decided to stay with it, and I enjoyed it so much that it became my main inspiration for changing my major to Electrical Engineering.

Looking to the future, I am excited about the opportunity to develop low-latency, real-time systems for a company whose mission and values closely align with my own. More specifically, I am looking for an environment where my daily work involves developing signal processing and embedded systems that reinforce American dominance in defense, aerospace, or technology.

Projects

Embedded Audio Processing Engine ↗

Real-time audio playback and recording device implemented on a RP2350 microcontroller, optimizing memory usage to support custom sound generation under constrained hardware resources. An I2C digital potentiometer was used for volume control, PWM for sound generation, ADC for custom sound recording, and SPI for displaying information about the sound being played.

C Embedded DSP Real-time
Maze Navigation Robot ↗

Designed and built an autonomous robot to navigate a maze using sensor feedback and control logic. Implemented navigation and path tracking algorithms in Python. The mechanical design featured an omnidirectional drivetrain that allowed for faster navigation, as the robot did not need to rotate to change directions.

Python Computer Vision Autonomous Systems
Algorithmic Trading Signal Processor (In Progress) ↗

C++ system applying DSP techniques such as FFT spectral analysis, FIR/IIR filters, and Kalman filtering to financial time-series data for signal extraction and noise rejection.
